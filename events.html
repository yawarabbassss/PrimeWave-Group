Standing Together For Early Detection & Life-Saving Care.

Primewave Group of Companies marked Pink October with an immersive, company-wide breast cancer awareness campaign. Our entire operations floor wore pink ribbons in solidarity, hosting informative health workshops led by qualified medical advisors. We proudly sponsored preventive clinical screenings and checkups for all female team members, reinforcing our ongoing commitment to employee wellness, community health, and dismantling awareness taboos.

"At Primewave, our people always come first. Early detection saves lives, and ensuring our team has both the knowledge and direct healthcare access is fundamental to who we are as a company."
